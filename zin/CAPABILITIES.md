# What Zin can do

Zin is a desktop helper who notices what is going on, decides what would help,
and does it, either on her own or after asking, depending on how risky it is.

## How she thinks

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Senses** | Watches for things worth acting on: a finished download, new email, low disk or battery, the app you are using, music playing, how long you have been away, reminders coming due |
| **Brain** | Rules that are always on and free. With the optional free local AI (Ollama, runs on your PC, nothing leaves it) she also understands plain English, plans multi-step jobs ("tidy my desktop and put the screenshots in a Screenshots folder") and writes friendlier messages. Without it she falls back to simple phrase matching |
| **Hands** | The actions below. The AI can only choose from these; it can't run anything else |
| **Memory** | Your choices, saved on your PC: where each kind of download goes, how you like folders sorted, favorite songs and apps, quiet hours. She learns from your answers so she asks less over time |

## How much she does on her own

| Level | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Just does it, then tells you** | Sorts a finished download into the folder you chose for its type, shows new-email alerts, reminders, timers, opening apps you ask for, playing music |
| **Asks first** | Deleting or moving more than a few files, cleaning old files and photos, closing apps, anything she has not done for you before |
| **Never** | Sending or deleting email, permanently deleting files (always the Recycle Bin), installing software, spending money, changing passwords or security settings |

She stays quiet while you are in a fullscreen game or video and during quiet
hours, and each kind of nudge has a cooldown so she doesn't nag.

## What she can do

### Files
- **Sort downloads automatically**: when a download finishes, she moves it to the folder you picked for its type (photos, documents, installers, music, video, archives). She waits for the download to finish completely, and asks the first time she sees a new type
- **Organize a folder on request**: by type, by date, alphabetically (A–Z folders) or by project name. She shows the plan first and keeps an undo
- **Clean up old files and photos**: finds files not changed in months, duplicate and near-duplicate photos, blurry or tiny images, old installers and big forgotten files. Everything goes to the Recycle Bin after you approve it
- **Find files** by name, type or date ("the PDF I downloaded yesterday"), then opens or shows them
- **File tools**: duplicate finder, bulk rename, zip and unzip, resize and convert images

### Apps and music
- **Open and close apps by name** ("open Discord", "close Chrome"), even with a slightly off name
- **Spotify**:
  - With Premium, after a one-time Spotify login, she plays any song, artist or playlist you name
  - With free Spotify, she opens Spotify on the song's search results
  - Play, pause, skip and volume work either way
- **System controls**: volume, brightness, dark mode, focus mode, lock the PC, screenshots

### Email
- **New-mail alerts**: "3 new emails, one from Sarah about Friday"
  - Works with Gmail and Outlook.com (through an app password, stored with Windows' encryption) or the Outlook desktop app
  - With the AI on, she can sum up what a batch of emails is about
- She only reads who emails are from and their subject lines (and a short preview if you allow it). She never sends, deletes or marks mail as read

### Time and reminders
- Reminders ("remind me at 3 to call mom"), timers, a 25-minute focus timer, a to-do list and quick notes
- Break nudges after long stretches at the PC

### PC care
- Warns when the disk, battery or memory is low and offers to fix it
- Junk cleanup (temp files, Recycle Bin, old installers)
- Startup apps you can turn off to make your PC start faster

### Web
- Google or YouTube searches and opening sites by name
- Weather from a free service, no account needed

### Personality
- Reacts to what you are doing: dances when music plays, types along when you code, dozes when you are away, cheers when a big job finishes
- Chats, jokes and keeps you company (smarter with the AI on)

## Every task uses her animations

She teleports to where the task is happening and works on her hologram keyboard
while the task runs. She points at results, cheers when she's done and shrugs if
something fails.
