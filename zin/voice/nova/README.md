# Nova

Zin's voice. These are clips of a voice made on ElevenLabs, one per mood, with
what's said in each (`lines.json`). ZipVoice copies the voice and feeling of the
clip for the mood she's in, so she talks like this on the PC for free, offline,
without ElevenLabs.

To change her voice, replace the clips with new ones (8 to 12 seconds each,
clean, no music) and update `lines.json` to match word for word.
