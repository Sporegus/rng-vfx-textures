# Emotion clips

Zin's emotional voices are made by the Pocket TTS model copying the voice and
mood of these short clips (8 seconds each). The clips are cut from the EARS
dataset (Richter et al., "EARS: An Anechoic Fullband Speech Dataset Benchmarked
for Speech Enhancement and Dereverberation", Interspeech 2024),
https://github.com/facebookresearch/ears_dataset, which is released under the
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license
(CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

**Non-commercial use only.** Zin may not be sold, or used in anything sold,
with these clips. To sell Zin, replace them with recordings you have the rights
to (a voice actor's, or your own), or use the classic voices.

The clips were trimmed, had pauses shortened, and were volume-normalized.

| Voice | EARS speaker | Moods (EARS emotion) |
|---|---|---|
| Mochi | p011 | cute (cuteness), happy (amusement), excited (amazement), sad (sadness), calm (serenity), confused (confusion), proud (pride), pouty (anger), shy (embarassment), love (adoration) |
| Pixie | p062 | same |
| Luna | p068 | same |

**Pixie** (`voice/styles/pixie.bin`) is a Kokoro voice found by searching blends
of Kokoro's own voices for the closest match to EARS speaker p062's recordings,
so it is also covered by these terms.
