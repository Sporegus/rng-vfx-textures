# Nova's recorded phrases

Short lines made once in her ElevenLabs voice and played for everyday moments,
so they sound exactly like ElevenLabs but cost nothing after they're made.
Anything else (chat, the weather, the time) is made on the spot as before.

`phrases.json` lists them by cue (what's happening, see `../../cues.js`):

    { "open": [{ "file": "open-1.wav", "text": "Opening it now!", "mood": "happy" }] }

A cue with no recording is simply said in full by her voice.
