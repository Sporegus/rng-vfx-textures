# Nova

Nova's voice. These are clips of a voice made on ElevenLabs, one per mood, with
what's said in each (`lines.json`). ZipVoice copies the voice and feeling of the
clip for the mood she's in, so she talks like this on the PC for free, offline,
without ElevenLabs.

To change her voice, replace the clips with new ones (8 to 12 seconds each,
clean, no music) and update `lines.json` to match word for word.

The clips have had a light clean-up (a little hiss taken out, nothing above
11.5 kHz), since she copies any noise in them too.

Cute, happy, pouty and proud are 9 to 13 second stretches cut from longer
recordings: of the stretches tried, these made her sound most like herself and
most like the mood of the whole recording. Longer clips copy better but make her
slower (she rereads the clip for every sentence), so about 10 seconds is the aim.
The old happy clip started with a laugh, which made her sound like someone else.
